About Keith West

Photographer & Retoucher based in Surrey and London

Hi, I'm Keith West. Hi, I'm Keith West. I create editorial, commercial, beauty, and motorsport imagery with a focus on storytelling, atmosphere, and technical precision. My work spans studio portraiture, fashion, automotive, and live motorsport coverage, combining strong visual style with a documentary approach to capturing movement, emotion, and detail. I operate from a dedicated studio space in Surrey while also covering events and locations across the UK, including circuit-based motorsport photography and automotive editorial work.

My Photography Journey

I began my photography career shooting live music and events, developing a passion for capturing energy, timing, and atmosphere in fast-moving environments. Over time, I transitioned into commercial and beauty photography, working with brands, models, creatives, and agencies to produce polished portfolio and campaign imagery. More recently, I’ve expanded into automotive and motorsport photography, covering GT racing and track events with a focus on dynamic action, cinematic composition, and editorial storytelling.
